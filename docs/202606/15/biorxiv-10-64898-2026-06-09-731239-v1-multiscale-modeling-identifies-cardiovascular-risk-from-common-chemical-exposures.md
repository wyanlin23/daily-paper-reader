## 摘要
背景：心血管系统受外源因素显著影响，但由于数据可用性有限，对药物和环境化学品所构成风险的理解受到限制。新方法学（NAMs）应用体外、化学和计算机方法表征危害和风险，提供基于人类生物学的策略以克服监管挑战，并补充或替代动物试验以理解化学物质对心血管的影响。方法：我们应用了一个工作流程，使用生理药代动力学（PBPK）模型，将来自超过300个具有心血管相关分子和细胞靶点的高通量筛选（HTS）测定的生物活性浓度转换为859种物质的人等效给药剂量（EADs）。为了推导风险预测，将体外活性衍生的EADs与人体暴露估计值和来自毒理学动物研究的体内出发点（PODs）进行比较。地理空间分析评估了人群水平风险。结果：结合HTS测定数据、人体暴露预测和PBPK建模，识别出在相关暴露水平下具有潜在心血管毒性的化合物。个人护理产品成分、阻燃剂、除草剂、杀虫剂、药物以及各种工业过程的副产品被列为优先关注的物质，它们优先靶向内皮细胞信号传导、核激素受体和其他关键心血管靶点。在评估的859种化学物质中，对于96.4%的化学物质，体外心血管相关测定比动物研究更具风险保护性。17种化学物质的log10生物活性暴露比（BER）低于-2，表明估计的人体暴露量超过体外衍生生物活性剂量的100倍以上。结论：本研究建立了一个整合的多尺度框架，将分子扰动与人群水平心血管风险联系起来，能够系统识别潜在的心脏毒性化学物质。通过将机制毒理学与药代动力学建模相结合，该方法增强了人类健康风险评估的生物学相关性和转化影响。这一可扩展、适应性强的框架支持及时的循证决策，并与日益增长的NAMs应用相一致，以推进心血管研究和疾病预防。

## Abstract
Background: The cardiovascular system is significantly affected by exogenous factors, but understanding the risks posed by pharmaceuticals and environmental chemicals is restricted due to limited data availability. New approach methodologies (NAMs) apply in vitro, in chemico, and in silico methods to characterize hazard and risk, offering human biology-based strategies to overcome regulatory challenges and complement or replace animal testing for understanding chemical cardiovascular effects. Methods: We applied a workflow using physiologically based pharmacokinetic (PBPK) models to convert bioactive concentrations from >300 high-throughput screening (HTS) assays with cardiovascular-relevant molecular and cellular targets to human equivalent administered doses (EADs) for 859 substances. To derive risk predictions, the in vitro activity-derived EADs were compared with human exposure estimates and in vivo points of departure (PODs) from toxicological animal studies. Geospatial analysis assessed population-level risks. Results: The combined HTS assay data, human exposure predictions and PBPK modeling identified compounds with potential cardiovascular toxicity at relevant exposure levels. Personal care product ingredients, flame retardants, herbicides, pesticides, pharmaceuticals, and byproducts of various industrial processes were noted as agents of concern preferentially targeting endothelial cell signaling, nuclear hormone receptors, and other critical cardiovascular targets. Of the 859 chemicals assessed, in vitro CV-relevant assays were more risk protective than animal studies for 96.4% of the chemicals. Seventeen chemicals had a log10 bioactivity exposure ratio (BER) below -2, indicating that the estimated human exposure >100-fold above the in vitro-derived bioactive dose. Conclusions: This study establishes an integrative, multiscale framework linking molecular perturbations to population-level cardiovascular risk, enabling systematic identification of potentially cardiotoxic chemicals. By bridging mechanistic toxicology with pharmacokinetic modeling, this approach enhances the biological relevance and translational impact of human health risk assessment. This scalable, adaptable framework supports timely, evidence-based decision-making and aligns with the growing adoption of NAMs to advance cardiovascular research and disease prevention.
