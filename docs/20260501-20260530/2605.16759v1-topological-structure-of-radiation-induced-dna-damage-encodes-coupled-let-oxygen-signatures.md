## 摘要
我们首次在临床粒子治疗范围内，对辐射诱导的DNA双链断裂（DSB）拓扑结构进行了核尺度持续同调与随机森林分类分析。利用TOPAS-nBio和体素感知氧模型，我们在49种条件（7种粒子构型，0.2--70.7 keV/μm；7个氧水平，0.005--21% O₂）下生成了2,450个模拟细胞核，并提取了7种模态下的107维特征矩阵。DSB拓扑结构以三个层次编码粒子身份、扩展布拉格峰（SOBP）位置和氧张力，每一层次的保真度由控制它的物理机制决定。粒子身份和SOBP位置可精确解码（平衡精度=1.000）。氧水平分类的精度随LET单调下降，从电子的0.517到碳远端SOBP的0.189，而在氦到碳的过渡中存在电荷驱动的非单调性，证实了原子序数（而非仅LET）主导拓扑可区分性。联合49类任务的平衡精度为0.346，是随机概率的17倍。各类召回率在0.5% O₂处普遍达到峰值（所有构型下0.788--0.976），这与氧增强比（OER）曲线拐点一致。拓扑摘要（持续熵、景观积分）在所有LET下主导氧编码（η²_O₂ = 0.300--0.622）。一项部分排除测试揭示了两个机制上可分离的通道：一个计数介导的尺度信号（η²_O₂生存比0.062）和一个与计数无关的形状信号，在7种构型中有5种得到保留或增强（平衡精度生存比1.011）。持续熵和景观积分作为新型放射生物学观测量，为在缺氧肿瘤治疗计划中表征氧依赖性损伤拓扑提供了计算基础。

## Abstract
We present the first nuclear-scale persistent homology and Random Forest classification analysis of radiation-induced DNA double-strand break (DSB) topology across the clinical particle therapy range. Using TOPAS-nBio and the Voxel-Aware Oxygen model, we generated 2,450 simulated nuclei across 49 conditions (seven particle configurations, 0.2--70.7~keV/\textmu{}m; seven oxygen levels, 0.005--21\%~O$_2$) and extracted a 107-feature matrix across seven modalities. DSB topology encodes particle identity, Spread-Out Bragg Peak (SOBP) position, and oxygen tension in a three-tier hierarchy, with fidelity at each tier governed by the physical mechanism controlling it. Particle identity and SOBP position are exactly decodable (balanced accuracy = 1.000). Oxygen-level classification degrades monotonically with LET from 0.517 (electrons) to 0.189 (carbon distal SOBP), with a charge-driven non-monotonicity at the helium-to-carbon transition confirming that atomic number, not LET alone, governs topological discriminability. The joint 49-class task achieves balanced accuracy 0.346, seventeen times above chance. Per-class recall peaks universally at 0.5\%~O$_2$ (0.788--0.976 across all configurations), which is consistent with the OER curve inflection. Topological Summaries (persistent entropy, landscape integrals) dominate oxygen encoding at all LET ($η^2_{O_2} =\,$0.300--0.622). A partial-out test reveals two mechanistically separable channels: a count-mediated scale signal ($η^2_{O_2}$ survival ratio 0.062) and a count-independent shape signal preserved or enhanced in five of seven configurations (balanced accuracy survival ratio 1.011). Persistent entropy and landscape integrals, as novel radiobiological observables, provide a computational basis for characterizing oxygen-dependent damage topology in hypoxic tumor treatment planning.
